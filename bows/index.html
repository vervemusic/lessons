About me

I've played the violin since 1962 and grew up in my father's luthier shop. I was Sales Manager at Stamell Strings in Amherst, helped open their Poughkeepsie shop, and trained at the UNH bow school. I was also Sales and Marketing Manager for Mando Mo Strings as a consultant.

I know the mandolin family like the back of my hand: mandolins, mandolas, mandocellos, octave mandolins and Irish bouzoukis. I know most of the brands and have visited the factories in South Korea and China where many of them are made.

I've followed bow hair to its source: the horse farms of Mongolia, a slaughterhouse in Harbin, then by train to the hair-processing town of Anping. I know where good hair comes from and what it takes to get it. Now I help players and shops directly, from home.
